## Product decision

Do not silently choose whichever source looks newer. Keep the complete 2025–26 dataset as an explicitly archived preview. Separate its data from verified current service. Show “Current schedule unconfirmed” by default and offer source links and historical exploration. Confirmation of a current timetable must include effective dates and service exceptions before enabling current-trip recommendations.

## Updating safely

1. Obtain a dated official schedule and applicable service calendar; resolve conflicting days with the operator/college.
2. Record URLs, effective date bounds, confirmation date and applicable exceptions in the dataset.
3. Compare each row and stop order with the source; retain midnight arrivals as offsets beyond 24:00.
4. Run engine and browser checks, including changed late days and final Saturday run.
5. Update service status only after the evidence supports it. GPS support additionally requires an authorized feed and measured freshness; timetable accuracy cannot establish vehicle location.
